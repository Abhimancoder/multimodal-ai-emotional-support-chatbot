# Verified Crisis Support Resources and Helplines

## 1. United States & Canada: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
- **Contact:** Call or Text **988**
- **Availability:** 24/7, Free, Confidential
- **Description:** The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is a national network of local crisis centers that provides free and confidential emotional support to people in suicidal crisis or emotional distress.

---

## 2. Crisis Text Line
- **Contact:** Text **HOME to 741741** (US & Canada) or **85258** (UK)
- **Availability:** 24/7, Free, Confidential
- **Description:** Free, 24/7 support for those in crisis. Connect with a volunteer crisis counselor via text message.

---

## 3. The Trevor Project (LGBTQ Youth)
- **Contact:** Call **1-866-488-7386** or Text **START to 678-678**
- **Availability:** 24/7, Free, Confidential
- **Description:** Crisis intervention and suicide prevention services for lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, and questioning (LGBTQ) young people.

---

## 4. Veterans Crisis Line
- **Contact:** Dial **988, then press 1**, or Text **838255**
- **Availability:** 24/7, Free, Confidential
- **Description:** Dedicated to military veterans, service members, National Guard, Reserve, and their families.

---

## 5. Global Emergency Numbers
- **Europe & India:** 112
- **United Kingdom:** 999
- **Australia:** 000
- **United States & Canada:** 911
